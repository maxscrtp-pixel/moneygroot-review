Number six. The weird-facts compilation, the laziest format here to actually produce. There is no story to build, you just stack short, surprising facts back to back. What Gives does it with animals, and animals with the most depressing lives did 839,000 views. Bend it into weird history and it goes nuclear, Sam O'Nella Academy did nearly ten million views on banned and controversial foods. Or into space, like Captain Cipher, whose weirdest things found on Mars did 581,000. The longevity is excellent, this kind of niche never runs out of facts, and strong thumbnails can carry one video for months. But here is the catch you have to respect. A pile of clips with a voiceover is exactly what YouTube flags as reused content, so your facts have to be genuinely re-written, your narration original, and your editing real, or you get demonetized. Make it yours and it prints. Phone it in and it dies.
